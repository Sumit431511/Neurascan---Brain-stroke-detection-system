Support

Frequently Asked
Questions

Can I use NeuraScan for clinical diagnosis?+
No. NeuraScan is for research and educational use only. Always seek professional medical evaluation.
Why is the threshold 0.35 and not 0.5?+
In medical screening, missing a real stroke (false negative) is far more dangerous than a false positive. The lower threshold prioritises sensitivity — catching more potential strokes at the cost of some specificity.
Is my scan stored anywhere?+
Scans are saved to the backend server to generate heatmaps and PDF reports. They are not shared with any third party. Only authorised doctors with a valid account can access prediction records.
The API returns 503 — what do I do?+
A 503 means the model weights file hasn't loaded. Place model_weights.npy in backend/models/ and restart the server. Look for "✅ Model loaded successfully" in the terminal.
